**Welcome to Ultinotes!** 👋 I am Adrian, and I care about empowering individuals through technology, my goal with this project is to create open-source tools that help people build a foundation for independent knowledge and productivity.

# Scope

Currently this involves building different plugins and integrations around Obsidian. Future goals will be to build tools and integrations for local AI, Web3, and the underlying developer tooling.

**Your contributions will definitely help, as my main limitation currently is time, rather than skill or motivation (I am a software developer after all).**

# Mission

At the heart of Ultinotes lies a commitment to preserving user privacy and control. In an era where data collection and surveillance are increasingly prevalent, I'm dedicated to building tools that respect your autonomy and allow you to maintain ownership over your digital life.

Beyond that, Ultinotes is designed to help you unlock your full potential. By creating intuitive, user-centric tools, my aim is to enable individuals to focus on what matters most – their passions, skills, and personal growth. If you want to support my work, consider becoming a sponsor.
